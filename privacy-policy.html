Privacy Policy

Effective date: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how Bachpan Garments (“we”, “us” or “our”) handles information when you visit the website, create an account, make a Test Mode payment, or contact us with a school-uniform enquiry. Please read it before submitting personal information.

1. Information we may collect

  • Enquiries: information you choose to send us by phone, email or WhatsApp, such as your name, contact details, school, requested items, sizes or measurements.
  • Accounts: your email address, parent/guardian name, mobile number and delivery address, plus a securely hashed password and email-verification/sign-in security records. Passwords are not stored in plain text.
  • Orders and Test Mode payments: your account ID, order reference, requested products, sizes, quantities, verified product subtotal, Razorpay order ID, payment ID, payment status and timestamps. Test Mode payments are simulated and do not move real money.
  • Technical information: information that a hosting provider or server may record for security and operation, such as IP address, browser/device details, request time, error/security logs, and Razorpay webhook event IDs and payload hashes used to prevent duplicate processing. Raw webhook bodies are not retained.
  • Browser storage and session cookies: the cart is kept in browser storage, while a necessary secure session cookie keeps you signed in and protects account, checkout and payment-verification requests.

Please do not send payment-card details, account passwords, identity documents or other unnecessary sensitive information through enquiry channels.

2. How we use information

We use account information to verify your email and provide sign-in; order and payment information to create orders, verify Test Mode payments, show order history, send receipts and notify the store; and enquiry information to respond, confirm product availability and sizing, provide customer support, and comply with applicable legal obligations. Test Mode payments are simulated, and orders do not reserve stock or confirm dispatch.

3. Payments and third-party services

Razorpay Standard Checkout processes payment details. In this Test Mode integration, the website receives payment and order identifiers and status; it does not store card numbers, CVVs, UPI credentials or other payment credentials. Razorpay's privacy terms apply to its service. WhatsApp processes information you send through its service, and the configured email provider sends account and payment receipts. We do not control external providers' privacy practices.

Our hosting provider processes account, order and payment-status records to operate the website. We may share information with Razorpay, email, communications or hosting providers as reasonably needed to create and verify Test Mode payments, deliver receipts and store notifications, respond to you, maintain security, or meet legal obligations.

4. When we disclose information

We do not sell personal information. We may disclose information to hosting or communications providers where needed for the purposes above, where you ask us to do so, or where disclosure is required to comply with law, respond to lawful requests, protect rights and safety, or investigate fraud or security incidents.

5. Retention and security

We keep account and order records while needed to provide account access, maintain your order history, resolve disputes, and meet legal requirements. Verification tokens expire after 30 minutes. You may request account or information deletion by contacting us; legal, accounting, or dispute records may need to be retained. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards. No website, transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to create an account, but adding products to the cart and checking out require a verified email account. You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to identity verification and any legal record-retention requirements.

7. Children’s information

Our products include school uniforms, but the website is intended to be used by customers and guardians arranging enquiries or purchases. If a child’s information is included in an enquiry, please provide only what is necessary to identify the requested uniform or sizing. A parent or guardian may contact us about information submitted on a child’s behalf.

8. External links and policy updates

This website may link to third-party services. Their websites and privacy policies are governed by those providers, not by this policy. We may update this policy when our services or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top indicates the latest published version.
